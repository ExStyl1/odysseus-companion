## What was taken

- `CraftAgentKit/` — the reusable Swift package: `Protocol/` (MessageEnvelope,
  ProtocolCodec, ProtocolConstants, RPCChannels, WireError, JSONValue),
  `Transport/RPCTransport`, `Client/RPCClient+*`, `Auth/` (KeychainStore,
  ServerConnection), and its tests.
- `CraftAgentsApp/` — the SwiftUI app: onboarding, session list, chat (including
  `PermissionApprovalSheet`, the reason this app exists), persistence,
  design system, `project.yml` for XcodeGen, and the view-model tests.
