## Decisions subsystem (analyst, wf_89c06b5c-ef3)
WHAT IT IS. An opt-in 'decision layer' that sends typed questions to Jev, TypeSafe AI's 'System One' classifier. The questions are 'choice' (2-255 options), 'score' (an ordered rubric of 2-10 levels) and 'noul' (yes/no probability). It answers with probabilities in about 0.1-0.5 s and never writes text. It is not an LLM call. It is off by default and gated only by the Settings switch (no env flag). When off, unsure, slow or failing, every caller returns null and the app behaves exactly as before. A decision never grants authority.

WHEN IT CAME. Two steps. v0.13.6 shipped the client, settings, the `decide` agent tool, the RPC and the Settings card. v0.14.0 added decision-point.ts, usage tracking, 13 feature toggles and the host features.

ENTRY POINTS.
(1) packages/shared/src/decisions/. client.ts (458 lines; SystemOneClient POSTs {base}/v1/systemone). providers.ts presets: typesafe (https://api.typesafe.ai, jev-1.13.0); openrouter (typesafe/jev-1.13, can reuse an OpenRouter LLM connection's key); vercel-ai-gateway (typesafe-ai/jev); laya (a local open-source server at http://127.0.0.1:8000 with a /health probe, 48 KB state cap); custom (a self-hosted Jev-compatible server). settings.ts stores StoredConfig.decisionLayer: enabled (default false), provider, connectionSlug, baseUrl, model, deadlineMs, and features with 13 toggles (decideTool, taskVerdicts, semanticLabels, turnOutcome, guardedMode, riskBadges, automationConditions, taskRepairs, smartTitles, adaptiveThinking, midTurnMessages, largeResults, suggestions). records.ts appends every call to ~/.craft-agent/logs/decisions.jsonl with a sha256 of the state, never the text. Also resolve.ts (switch, then toggle, then key, then endpoint), status.ts, health.ts, usage.ts and types.ts.
(2) packages/server-core/src/decisions/. decision-point.ts: openDecisionPoint({feature, record}) applies the toggle gate, a background deadline (FOREGROUND_MAX_DEADLINE_MS 3000), recording and recordDecisionOutcome/FollowUp. Then one module per feature: task-verdict, task-repairs, turn-outcome, semantic-labels, automation-condition, guarded-mode, permission-risks, smart-titles, adaptive-thinking, mid-turn-messages, large-results and suggestions, plus tool-callbacks.ts for `decide`.
(3) The `decide` session tool (session-tools-core handlers/decide.ts plus a DecideSchema in tool-defs). It is a registry tool, readOnly, safeMode allow, accepts up to 200 items and 20 questions, and is hidden unless includeDecide is passed.
(4) agent/core/guarded-mode.ts (211 lines) adds a new 'guarded' PermissionMode between Ask and Execute. Non-read-only Bash, MCP writes and non-GET API calls are checked by the model and turned into a prompt when risky. Writes outside the cwd or the plans/data folders always ask. When the layer is off, Guarded behaves like Ask.
(5) SessionManager wiring, roughly 900 interleaved lines: guardedModeCheck, risk badges on permission_request, applyTurnOutcome (moves a session to Needs Review), smart titles, mid-turn steer-or-queue with merged replays, suggestions, adaptive thinking, and the semanticCondition gate next to the automation loop guard.
(6) rpc/decisions.ts with 7 channels decisions:{getSettings, setSettings, getStatus, setApiKey, deleteApiKey, test, probeServer}, all REMOTE_ELIGIBLE. A credential type decision_api_key::{provider}.
(7) UI: AiSettingsPage +428 lines (a 'Decision model' card with the provider, a key or connection reuse, a Laya health check, a connection test and 'Advanced settings' toggles in 4 groups); atoms/permission-modes and useAvailablePermissionModes; risk badges in PermissionRequest; a decide overlay in tool-parsers and TurnCard.
(8) i18n: about 95 settings.ai.decisions.* keys, plus mode.guarded*, chat.permissionRisk.*, tasks.verdictInferred, automations.skippedSummary and table.semanticRule. Docs: resources/docs/decisions.md. A scripts/decisions-report.ts.
Size: about 4.5k new source lines and 2.6k test lines, excluding the SessionManager hunks.
