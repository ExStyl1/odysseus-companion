**Method.**
- Every item: upstream read by tag diff, then ours read at HEAD (CodeGraph/`git grep`/`rg`, file:line quoted).
- Every absence claim names its positive control on the same instrument.
- Claude claims come from the docs pages, curled raw on 2026-10-14 (`tools-reference.md`, `mcp.md`). Codex claims come from our vendored `packages/codex-protocol/generated/v2` and from the codex-source citations already recorded in our `pool-server.ts`.
- Renderer files that also carry the redesign: only their FUNCTIONAL hunks are judged here. Visual churn is the redesign analyst's.

**Headline.**
- **Three CRITICAL defects are present in our shipped code.**
  - B4-09: our project-memory prompt instructs Write/Edit rewrites (data loss).
  - B4-12: the credential refresh single-flight is keyed by slug only (another workspace's token can be returned).
  - B4-16: the Generate prompt promises that `depends_on` passes output, and our TaskRunner does not pass it.
- **The upstream TaskRunner headline is mostly already handled in ours.** "Stopping a step marked it done with no output" does NOT happen here: F1.1239 folds a host Stop to `interrupted`, and header item 9 pauses the run. What remains is the stop-to-steer flow, the verify-turn guards and the `depends_on` handover.
- **MCP resources and server instructions are already native-first on all three runtimes in ours.** Only templates, pagination and the model-callable prompt tools differ.
